A test boat for the whole system

A 5.5 m aluminium airboat with an engine converted from the Nissan HR12DDR, built for about 175 hp and run by our Cat-GDI8 engine control unit. All our electronics are installed on board.

We are a young company with long experience behind it: more than 10 years of designing airboats and more than 5 years of engine management and electronics. On this boat it all comes together.

The boat also shows what we can do: hull, powertrain and electronics designed by one team. We do the same for our customers — from fitting our systems to your boat to developing electronics from scratch.

One control system for the whole boat: dashboard, engine control, power management and software working as one. LEGION is in development, and this boat is where it is built and tested.

AMg5 aluminium hull

The 5.5 m hull is made of AMg5 — an aluminium–magnesium alloy used in boat building. The boat is designed by our team in 3D, down to the engine frame.

Sprung seats for the driver and the passenger are moved back from the centre — the boat rides comfortably on waves at high speed. A compact steering console keeps the weight down.

Powertrain

Nissan HR12DDR, converted

An engine built for the airboat around our Cat-GDI8 engine control unit.

The engine is converted from the Nissan HR12DDR — a compact 1.2 L aluminium three-cylinder with direct injection. The factory supercharger is replaced with a Garrett 28 turbocharger, and the target output is about 175 hp.

The engine is run by our Cat-GDI8 engine control unit and set up through BrainBus. This is where our engine management meets real load: the propeller, vibration and water.

  • TurbochargingSupercharger replaced with a Garrett 28 turbo.
  • Light engineAluminium engine weighing just over 80 kg.
  • Own cooling systemDesigned for the airboat, with flaps that open when cooling is needed.
  • Belt reduction driveMade in-house, ratio 2.78 : 1. A belt runs quieter than gears; the drive is connected through a coupling.
