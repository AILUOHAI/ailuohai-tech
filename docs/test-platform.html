A test boat for the whole system

A 5.5 m aluminium airboat with an engine converted from the Nissan HR12DDR, built for about 175 hp and run by our Cat-GDI8 engine control unit. All our electronics are installed on board.

We are a young company with long experience behind it: more than 10 years of designing airboats and more than 5 years of engine management and electronics. On this boat it all comes together.

The boat also shows what we can do: hull, powertrain and electronics designed by one team. We do the same for our customers — from fitting our systems to your boat to developing electronics from scratch.

LEGION

One control system for the whole boat: dashboard, engine control, power management and software working as one. LEGION is in development, and this boat is where it is built and tested.

In the workshop

Top view

01

Built like a construction kit

An all-aluminium, light two-seat airboat with sprung seats. Its main idea is modularity: the steering console, the seats and the whole powertrain are removable, so the boat can be assembled the way you need it.

02

Welded aluminium hull

The hull is welded from AMg5 aluminium–magnesium alloy, together with the propeller guard.

03

Lightweight frame

The frame inside the hull is lightened as far as possible: every part that does not carry load is cut out.

04

Empty hull

An open hull without anything installed: everything — console, seats, powertrain — mounts onto the frame. The bottom is lined with PE500 polyethylene to protect the hull on shallow rivers, stones, grass and swamps.

05

Reinforced guard

The mounts of the propeller guard at the stern are reinforced, and so is the guard itself: it takes branches, trees and heavy impacts.

Powertrain

Engine

01

Nissan HR12DDR engine

The base is the Nissan HR12DDR — a compact 1.2 L aluminium three-cylinder with direct injection, weighing just over 80 kg.

02

Garrett GT2860 turbocharger

The factory supercharger is replaced with a modified Garrett GT2860 turbo. A Volkswagen electric actuator drives the wastegate, so boost is controlled precisely and limited exactly.

03

Intake manifold and intercooler

The intake manifold is redesigned and a new intercooler is made, with the shortest possible path from the turbo to the cylinder head — boost builds fast, without lag.

04

Belt reduction drive

The lightest possible belt reduction drive, designed for this engine. Quality bearings are rated for heavy, long-term load, and every bearing unit is closed with seals. A coupling damps vibrations between the propeller and the engine.

05

60-litre tank with two pumps

The 60-litre fuel tank holds two pumps that switch over automatically or by hand. This keeps the required fuel pressure in the rail and always gives an emergency backup pump. Two light gel batteries are installed here as well.

06

Aviation-style cooling

The cooling system is built the aviation way: the radiator sits inside the cowling, in a special diffuser. The propeller gets cleaner airflow and less shadowing. The main task it solves: in winter the engine does not freeze and warms up faster, in summer it does not overheat — a flap opens and closes the radiator as needed.

07

Built-in load control unit

The powertrain is fully self-contained and disconnects from the boat: it only needs the ignition signal and the command to start. Over CAN it reports every fault and open circuit to the Dash-6.8 — pumps, actuators, engine control units, ground connections and everything else installed.

08

About 175 hp

The target output is about 175 hp. The engine is run by our Cat-GDI8 engine control unit and set up through BrainBus.
