Test platform

We are building our own test platform to check every system in real conditions before it reaches you.

A test platform for the whole system

On the platform our electronics work as one: the Dash-6.8 instrument panel, the power distribution module (PDM) and the engine control unit share one CAN bus. We test them together — debugging, fault handling and how convenient the system is to use.

The second goal is tuning turbocharged direct-injection (GDI) engines: we want engines that are light, powerful and economical at the same time.

Our aim is to get the most out of the whole system: maximum economy, performance, reliability and ergonomics.

The platform also shows the way forward: the level of technology we want to bring our customers to. We take you there step by step — from fitting our systems to your vehicle to developing electronics from scratch.

What we test

Electronics in one networkInstrument panel, PDM and engine control unit on one CAN bus: data exchange, fault reports and the reaction to failures.
Software infrastructureSetup, debugging, logs and firmware updates through BrainBus: how clear the faults are and how easy the system is to use.
Turbocharged GDI enginesTuning direct injection with boost to get engines that are light, powerful and economical.
Engine and propellerEngine calibration under the real propeller load, cooling, vibration and long running.
Artificial intelligenceEvery run is recorded. We plan to use this data for AI: log analysis, early fault detection and help with setup.

Powertrain

Engine

01

A 1.2 L engine as the base

The base is a 1.2 L Nissan engine with direct injection — compact, aluminium and light. We improve it so that the electronics can show what the engine is capable of.

02

Turbocharger with electric boost control

The factory supercharger is replaced with a turbocharger. Its wastegate is driven by an electric actuator controlled by the engine control unit, so boost is set and limited precisely.

03

Intake manifold and intercooler

The intake manifold is redesigned and a new intercooler is made, with the shortest possible path from the turbo to the cylinder head — boost builds fast, without lag.

04

Belt reduction drive

A light belt reduction drive designed for this engine, with sealed bearing units and a coupling that damps vibrations between the propeller and the engine.

05

Two fuel pumps under control

Two pumps in the tank switch over automatically or by hand: the fuel pressure in the rail is always kept, and there is always a backup pump.

06

Cooling run by the electronics

The radiator sits inside the cowling, and a flap opens and closes it as needed: in winter the engine warms up fast, in summer it does not overheat.

07

Cat-GDI4 engine control unit

Torque-based control of the turbocharged direct-injection engine, auto-start and diagnostics: every fault is checked and reported to the Dash-6.8 over CAN.

08

Power distribution module (PDM)

The built-in PDM switches and protects the pumps, actuators and control units and reports every fault and open circuit over CAN. The powertrain needs only the ignition signal and the command to start.

09

What we aim for

A light, powerful and economical engine with reliable communication, clear diagnostics and fault reports — run by our Cat-GDI4 and set up through BrainBus.
