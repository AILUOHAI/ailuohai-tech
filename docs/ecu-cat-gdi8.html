ECU Cat-GDI8

Engine control unit for V8 direct-injection (GDI) engines in airboats. One processor core runs the engine, the second handles communication, logging and supervision.

Overview

Cat-GDI8 controls direct injection, ignition and the high-pressure fuel pump of a V8 engine. The engine core handles crank and cam timing, fuel and ignition, electronic throttle, high-pressure pump and knock control.

The communication core handles Ethernet, CAN, diagnostics and data logging to onboard memory, and supervises the engine core.

Key functions

  • Direct-injection control for 8 cylinders and two high-pressure fuel pump valves.
  • 8 ignition outputs: coil drivers and 5 V logic outputs for coils with built-in igniter.
  • 4 H-bridge outputs for electronic throttle and DC actuators.
  • Two knock sensor channels, VR crank input, SENT, analog and digital inputs.
  • Two CAN buses and LIN: works together with Dash-6.8 and other onboard modules.
  • Setup, live data and logs over Ethernet in the BrainBus GDI-8 application.

Development status

First prototype boards are assembled. Firmware is verified with automated tests and an ECU simulator; testing on a running engine is the next stage.

Specifications may change before release. For project inquiries, write to info@ailuohai-tech.com.
