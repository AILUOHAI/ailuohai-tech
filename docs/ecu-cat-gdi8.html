ECU Cat-GDI8

Engine control unit for V8 engines with hybrid injection: every cylinder has a direct injector and a port injector. Four cam phasers, fast start and torque-based control in one unit.

Capabilities

One unit controls fuel, air, ignition and valve timing of a V8 engine, and records everything it does.

Hybrid injection

8 direct (GDI) injectors and 8 port injectors. Direct injection with 1–3 pulses per cycle and a high-voltage current profile; the share of port and direct fuel is set in the calibration.

16 injectors · 8 GDI + 8 PFI

High-pressure fuel

Two high-pressure fuel pump valves, one per bank, with closed-loop rail pressure control.

2 HPFP · 2 rails

Variable valve timing

Four camshafts — intake and exhaust on each bank — with four cam phasers. Closed-loop control, oil pressure check, chain and phaser diagnostics, automatic tuning.

4 camshafts · 4 phasers

Fast start

The unit finds the engine position from the camshaft shape before the crank wheel gap arrives, so the first injection and spark come sooner after cranking starts.

Sync in ~190° of crank on average, vs ~310° by the gap

Flexible trigger

Crank wheels 36-1, 60-2, 36-2-2-2 or a cam-only wheel; camshaft shapes are described in the setup tool and checked with the built-in trigger logger.

Up to 4 camshaft inputs

Ignition

Eight outputs for passive 12 V coils with adaptive dwell, or 5 V signals for coils with a built-in igniter.

8 coils · individual timing and knock control

Throttles and wastegates

Four H-bridge outputs for one or two electronic throttles and electric wastegates. End-stop learning and automatic PID tuning; SENT position sensors supported.

4 H-bridges

Torque-based control

Air and torque model, lambda control, two knock sensor channels, wideband lambda and exhaust temperature from a CAN module. Pressures are sampled in sync with crank angle.

2 knock channels · wideband + EGT over CAN

Safety by design

Injection and spark come from hardware timers, never from a data bus. Three levels of supervision with an independent watchdog; on a fault the outputs are switched off. Fault codes in OBD format.

3-level supervision

Setup and logging

Calibration, live data and logs over Ethernet in BrainBus GDI-8. Calibrations are stored with rollback; logs are written to onboard memory. A built-in ECU simulator lets you prepare a calibration before the engine runs.

Ethernet · 2 × CAN · LIN

How it works

The unit has two processor cores. One runs the engine in real time: engine position, fuel, ignition, throttles, high-pressure pumps, cam phasers and knock control. The other handles Ethernet, CAN, diagnostics and logging, and supervises the first one.

The engine setup — trigger wheels, camshafts, injectors, coils, sensors — is done in the BrainBus GDI-8 application, without changing firmware.

Development status

First prototype boards are assembled. Firmware is verified with automated tests and an ECU simulator; testing on a running engine is the next stage. Boost control is planned.

Specifications may change before release. For project inquiries, write to info@ailuohai-tech.com.
