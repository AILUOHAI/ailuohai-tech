Overview

Dedicated engine control unit for airboat applications, with integrated diagnostics, CAN communication and ruggedised packaging for marine-style operation.

Key functions

  • Fuel and ignition control for configured engine setup.
  • Sensor acquisition and fault monitoring.
  • CAN communication with dashboard and future power module.
  • Calibration and firmware update workflow support.
  • Designed for integration into complete vessel electronics architecture.

Application

Designed for airboats and vessels with non-standard operating conditions, including moisture exposure, vibration and integration of multiple onboard electronic modules.
