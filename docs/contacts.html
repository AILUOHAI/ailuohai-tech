Contacts

Electronics, engine control units and software for airboats — from Shenzhen to customers worldwide.

AILUOHAI-TECH is a young company with long experience behind it. Its founder has been designing airboats for more than 10 years, and engine management systems and electronics for more than 5. Now we work as a full cycle: design, development, production, integration and support.

Our headquarters is in Shenzhen, China (Shenzhen Ailuohai Power Technology Co., Ltd.). We develop dashboards, engine control units, power control and the BrainBus software, and we work with customers all over the world: we connect to your system remotely, set it up and help with calibration.
