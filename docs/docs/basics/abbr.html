Abbreviations

TDC, MAP, PFI, EGT and other terms explained.

Ctrl K
EngineFuel and mixtureSensors and signalsControlUnit and communication

Engine

ICEInternal combustion engine A piston engine.
TDCTop dead centre The highest piston position, the reference for angles.
BDCBottom dead centre The lowest piston position.
CrankshaftCrankshaft The shaft the unit uses to track engine angle.
SASpark advance How many degrees before TDC the spark fires.
MBTMaximum Brake Torque The spark angle that gives the highest torque.
IdleIdle Engine running with no torque request from the lever.
EfficiencyEfficiency The share of energy turned into useful work.
VEVolumetric Efficiency How much air a cylinder takes relative to its volume.
rlRelative filling Air mass in the cylinder relative to nominal, in percent.

Fuel and mixture

GDIGasoline Direct Injection Fuel is injected directly into the cylinder.
PFIPort Fuel Injection Fuel is injected into the intake port before the valve.
HPFPHigh-pressure fuel pump Builds pressure in the GDI rail.
AFRAir-Fuel Ratio The mass ratio of air to fuel.
λLambda Mixture relative to ideal: 1 is ideal, below 1 is rich, above 1 is lean.
WBOWideband oxygen sensor Measures λ over a wide range.
E8585% ethanol fuel A blend of ethanol and gasoline.
SOIStart Of Injection The angle at which injection begins.

Sensors and signals

MAPManifold Absolute Pressure Absolute pressure in the intake manifold.
CLTCoolant temperature The main indicator of engine warm-up.
EGTExhaust Gas Temperature The temperature of the exhaust gases.
NTCNegative Temperature Coefficient A thermistor whose resistance falls as it heats up.
R25Resistance at 25 °C The anchor point of a thermistor curve.
βThermistor beta coefficient Shows how quickly resistance falls with heat, in kelvin.
VRVariable Reluctance An inductive shaft position sensor with two wires.
SENTSingle Edge Nibble Transmission A one-wire digital sensor protocol.
RMSRoot mean square A measure of the strength of an alternating signal.
Short circuitShort circuit A circuit shorted to ground or supply.

Control

ETBElectronic Throttle Body A motor-driven throttle.
PWMPulse-width modulation Controls average power by the on-time fraction.
PIDProportional-integral-derivative controller Drives the difference between target and actual to zero.
PIProportional-integral controller A PID without the derivative part.

Unit and communication

ECUEngine control unit The unit that controls the engine.
BatteryBattery The 12 V on-board supply.
CANController Area Network A communication bus between units.
OBD-II / OBDOn-Board Diagnostics The standard for diagnostics and fault codes.
NMEA 2000 / NMEAMarine communication standard The bus for marine instruments.
