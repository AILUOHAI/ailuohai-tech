Protecting the engine when the propeller unloads

Ctrl K

Rev limiter and propeller protection

In progress An unloaded propeller can over-rev the engine almost instantly, so the limiter works ahead: it takes shaft acceleration into account and removes torque smoothly in a band below the limit, quickly with spark, then with air. Emergency fuel cut on extreme over-rev is a separate setting and is off by default.

In progress Torque rises through a soft zone that takes up driveline backlash, then faster. Rise and fall rates are curves over rpm.

Planned A drive model (engine, gearbox, propeller), damping of torsional oscillations with spark timing, and a keep-out band around the driveline resonance.
