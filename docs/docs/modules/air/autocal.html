Fill calibration

“Air (filling)” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application
Planned. This function is described in advance; part of it is not implemented yet.

Purpose

Fill auto-calibration is still in development; the tab says In development. This page describes how to measure the filling maps by hand and what the app will do.

How it works

There is no air flow sensor, so air is calculated from fuel and the wideband sensor: air equals fuel times 14.7 times the measured lambda. The filling accuracy is therefore the accuracy of the injectors and the wideband sensor. At one speed and one cam position, change the intake pressure with the throttle, and with a turbo with boost: at least three points from 40 kPa to full. At each point wait until lambda and speed settle, about 2-3 s, and record the pressure and the air corrected to 20 °C. A line through the points gives the slope and the residual gas: the residual gas is the pressure where the line crosses zero. Example at 3000 rpm: 40 kPa gives 278 mg, 70 kPa 543 mg, 100 kPa 794 mg. That is a slope of 8.6 mg/kPa and residual gas of 7.8 kPa. If the points stray from the line by more than 2-3 %, check injector small pulses, wideband warm-up, knock and exhaust protection. Planned: the app collects the points itself once running is steady, fits the line, shows the scatter and writes the cell, and fills neighbouring cells by interpolation. Long-term wideband adaptation will be switched off during that time.

Setup

  1. OrderInjectors, then sensors, then cams in the zero position, intake cam steps, exhaust cam, charge heating and finally the throttle.
  2. Switch off adaptationIn the Fuel & mixture module switch off long-term wideband adaptation during the measurements, otherwise it hides the model error.
  3. Hold the speedLoad comes from the propeller in the water or a dyno; the throttle changes the intake pressure.
  4. Record the pointsIn the logger record intake pressure, fuel per cycle and lambda in steady running.
  5. Move into the mapsEnter slope and residual gas into the speed and cam cell in the Cylinder filling section.
