Cylinder filling

“Air (filling)” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

This section describes how much air the cylinder takes per cycle. Fuel, torque and spark timing are all calculated from this mass.

How it works

The unit does not use a volumetric efficiency table. At constant speed and cam positions the air in the cylinder rises with intake pressure almost along a straight line, only shifted: part of the volume is taken by residual gas. So two numbers are enough for each speed and intake cam position. The slope says how many milligrams of air each kPa of intake pressure adds. The residual gas is the pressure at which no fresh air enters the cylinder any more. Two pressure points set the whole line, from idle to boost. The exhaust cam correction multiplies the slope, 1.0 means no effect. Charge heating covers the air being heated by the walls: the heating share by speed moves the charge temperature from the air temperature towards the coolant temperature. Factory values: slope about 7.9 mg/kPa at 500 rpm and about 9.1 at 4500, residual gas from 6 to 10 kPa by intake cam, heating from 0.25 at 500 rpm to 0.11 at 7500. For comparison, an ideal cylinder of a 6.2 L V8 at 20 °C takes about 9.2 mg/kPa. Filling in percent is relative to a cylinder full of air at 0 °C and 101.3 kPa. The pressure for each cylinder is predicted to its intake stroke; the prediction limit is set in the Sensors module, intake pressure section. The same model also works backwards: from the required air mass the unit finds the intake pressure the throttle must give.

Setup

  1. Prepare injectors and sensorsAir is calculated from fuel and the wideband sensor, so first you need accurate injector data, intake pressure, temperatures and a warm wideband sensor.
  2. Measure slope and residual gasAt one speed and one cam position record at least three pressure points; the procedure is in the Fill calibration section.
  3. Go through the cam positionsRepeat for the intake cam positions, then set the exhaust cam correction.
  4. Set the charge heatingThe same point on a cold and on a hot engine must give the same air.
  5. SavePress Save to ECU.
