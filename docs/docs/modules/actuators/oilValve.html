Oil pressure valve

“Actuators” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

The valve controls the oil pump pressure. This section sets up its output, feed-forward and controller.

How it works

The valve can be PWM, for smooth pressure, or two-position. PWM starts from a feed-forward curve, and a PI controller on the oil pressure sensor removes the remainder, with battery voltage correction. The logic that picks pressure by engine state is not connected yet, so in operation the valve stays in the safe position with the highest pressure. On an open circuit, a short or if pressure is not held, the valve also goes to the safe position until reset.

Setup

  1. Open the sectionActuators module, Oil pressure valve section.
  2. Choose the kindPWM or two-position, and what happens without current.
  3. Choose the outputPWM needs LS1, LS2, LS7 or LS8; two-position can use any free LS1…LS8.
  4. Check by handSet the PWM and watch the pressure. Manual mode lasts 30 s.
  5. Run auto-tuningEngine running steadily, oil warm, pressure sensor connected.
  6. SavePress Save to ECU.

What is in the application

Actuators → Oil pressure valve

Valve

Output, kind, PWM, live pressure and duty, check by hand, auto-tuning.

Feed-forward

PWM by the target pressure at the nominal voltage - the regulator starts from it. Filled by the auto-tuning («Valve» tab).

Regulator

PI on the oil pressure sensor, PWM limits, battery correction.

Errors

Valve circuit and «does not hold the pressure».
