Configuration

“Engine” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

All setup starts with this section. It describes the physical engine: cylinders, layout, air and boost, fuel, ignition and camshafts. From these answers the app turns on the other modules, sections and tabs, so fill it in first. Right after it, set the table axes: many tables are tied to them, and without them there is no point in tuning anything else.

How it works

The Engine tab is built in parts. Cylinders: number (up to 8, as many as the injector and ignition channels allow), layout, V angle (30…180°), firing order from the typical ones or your own, displacement. Air and boost: turbocharging turns on the Boost module and the wastegate in Actuators (pneumatic or servo), two throttles - one per bank. Fuel: GDI only or hybrid injection (turns on the PFI injectors section), one or two high-pressure pumps, one or two rails, which camshaft drives the pump. Ignition: passive 12 V coils (the ECU measures the coil current, the dwell is learned) or coils with a built-in igniter, 5 V. Camshafts: number of cam phasers and the oil pressure valve. Options that do not fit the ECU equipment are grey with the reason. On the right is the engine seen from the front for the chosen configuration. Model tab: segment lead (how many degrees before the cylinder TDC its calculation starts, 400° by default), displacement for the filling model and fuel stoichiometry (petrol 14.7, E85 9.8). Segment lead and displacement change only with the engine stopped. The honest state: the configuration is kept in the app for now. Only some parameters go to the ECU: displacement, number of rails, coil type, pumps on. The ECU cylinder count is set separately, and if it differs from the configuration the app shows it. Keeping the whole configuration in the ECU is the next step. The Errors & limits tab (check of channels, firing order and sensors) is planned.

Setup

  1. Open the sectionEngine module, Configuration section.
  2. Describe the cylindersNumber, layout, V angle, firing order from the engine documentation, displacement.
  3. Describe air and fuelBoost, throttles, injection, pumps and rails.
  4. Describe ignition and camshaftsCoil type, cam phasers, oil pressure valve.
  5. Check the modelModel tab: displacement and fuel stoichiometry.
  6. SavePress Save to ECU.
  7. Next - the axesTable axes section: set them before any table.
