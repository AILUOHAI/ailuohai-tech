Spark and knock

Spark is calculated for the required torque. Knock in a cylinder window gives a fast retard with delayed recovery, the average retard feeds adaptation, and an octane factor blends the 92 and 100 maps. Retard causes no torque dip: air smoothly makes up the torque.
