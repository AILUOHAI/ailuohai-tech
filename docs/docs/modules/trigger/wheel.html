Wheel

“Trigger” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksTrigger wheelSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

This section teaches the unit the shape of the crank and cam wheels and ties them to cylinder 1 TDC. Afterwards the unit knows the exact engine angle and which of the two revolutions of the cycle is running.

How it works

The unit recognises a tooth not by absolute time but by the ratio of neighbouring intervals, which barely depends on rpm. The gap gives a large ratio and becomes the reference point. After sync each interval is compared with the expected one, corrected for shaft acceleration, so the speed wave from compression does not break the count. The crankshaft cannot tell the two revolutions of a cycle apart; the cam shape does that. If a camshaft gives no phase within 4 revolutions, the unit assumes a phase and while cranking switches it every 3 s until the engine catches.

Setup

  1. Prepare the enginePress “Switch the fuel off”, disconnect the coil supply, remove all spark plugs. Battery charged.
  2. Record the crankshaftCrankshaft tab, “Without plugs” step: “Autostart” and 4 seconds on the starter.
  3. Confirm the wheelCompare the detected wheel with the disc and press “Confirm wheel”. If none is found, enter it under “Manual input”.
  4. Capture the camshaftsCamshafts tab, same cranking, then “Apply shape”. Cam phasers must be in their home position.
  5. Crank with plugsFit all plugs, fuel and coils stay off. “With all plugs” step; if values leave the thresholds, press “Widen thresholds”.
  6. Find TDCTDC tab: one plug, record, “Apply TDC”. A pressure sensor in place of the plug is more accurate.
  7. Check with a timing lightMove TDC with the ±0.1° and ±1° buttons until the pulley mark lines up.
  8. Write to the ECUWith the engine stopped, press “Write to ECU”. When all steps are done, “Save kit” lights up.
