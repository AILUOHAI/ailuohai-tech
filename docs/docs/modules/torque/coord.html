Coordination

“Torque & limits” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

This section sets the torque the lever asks for and how fast the engine reaches it.

How it works

A map turns lever position and speed into shaft torque. The losses from the torque model are added to give the indicated target. While idle control works, its regulator replaces the lever request, and a slightly moved lever cannot give less than idle. Then protection limits and the speed limiter take the minimum. Limits never cut below the torque idle needs, so that the engine does not stall. Every torque rise starts softly: the first 30 Nm go at 150 Nm/s to take up the propeller drive backlash, then 2000 Nm/s. Torque falls at 3000 Nm/s. The speed limiter takes torque at once, without this smoothing. An air lead of 0.7 means: the throttle moves ahead by 70 % of the gap to the target, while spark holds the smooth rise. When the rise reaches the target, the air is already there. The factory lever map: about 540 Nm at full lever at 4200 rpm; towards a small lever the request falls faster than a straight line.

Setup

  1. Check the leverIn the Sensors module, 0 and 100 % of the lever must match the stops.
  2. Set the mapThe request rises with the lever without dips. A small lever gives small torque so the boat is easy to handle at low speed.
  3. Set the soft zoneA jerk when pulling away means the soft zone is too small or its rate too high.
  4. Check responseA lazy response is cured by the rise rate and the air lead.
  5. SavePress Save to ECU.
