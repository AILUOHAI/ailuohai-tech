Torque & limits

Main model: air, torque, coordination, limits (smooth torque only).

Ctrl K

Torque structure

The lever and idle controller request torque, limits take the minimum, and two paths deliver it: air slowly and spark quickly. That is why limits are smooth, with no cuts.

Torque reserve

More air is held than needed and late spark absorbs the excess. When torque is needed at once, spark returns to optimum within one cycle, without waiting for the manifold to fill.

Stepped protections

Each cause gives only a torque fraction from 0 to 1, and the strictest applies. The lowest step is a get-home mode, not zero.

Speed governor

Experimental mode, off by default: the lever sets rpm and the unit finds the torque, with propeller feed-forward.
