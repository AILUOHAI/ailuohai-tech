Buses

“CAN” module: what it is, how it works, how to set it up.

Ctrl K
PurposeHow it worksSetupTroubleshootingWhat is in the application

Purpose

The section sets the speed of the two CAN buses and what works on them: an OBD-II scanner, the Dash 6.8 panel and an NMEA 2000 marine display.

How it works

By default CAN1 runs at 500 kbit/s: lambda and exhaust temperature modules, the OBD-II scanner and the dash. CAN2 runs at 250 kbit/s: NMEA 2000 to the marine display. The ECU receives the lambda modules on both buses; if they are on a bus that is not at 500 kbit/s, the app warns. The ECU checks the rules itself and will not accept a wrong setting: NMEA 2000 works only at 250 kbit/s, OBD-II at 500 or 250, each role on one bus only. So when a role is switched on, the app sets the right speed and removes the role from the other bus. Both buses have a 120 Ω terminator on the board, so the ECU must be at the end of the bus. On an NMEA 2000 backbone the ECU is a drop, and its terminator must be removed, otherwise the bus has three terminators. Listen only (in the detailed view) receives frames without acknowledging and sends nothing - to study a foreign bus. CAN has not been checked on the board yet: work with the dash, a scanner and a marine display has only been checked on the simulator.

Setup

  1. Open the sectionCAN module, Buses section.
  2. Turn on the busesTurn on the buses that have something connected.
  3. Assign the rolesTurn on OBD-II, dash and NMEA 2000 on the right buses: the speed is set by itself.
  4. Check the terminatorsECU at the end of the bus - terminator needed; ECU as a drop on an NMEA 2000 backbone - remove the terminator.
  5. SavePress Save to ECU.
